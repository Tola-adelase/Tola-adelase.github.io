Behind the project: data, methods & limitations

The question

How can an interactive tool make different contributions in the same match easier to explore, while keeping the context visible?

Source and preparation

StatsBomb Open Data: final 3869685 and semi-finals 3869519 / 3869552. A Python script reads events and lineups from a pinned source revision, checks unique event IDs, excludes shootouts and goalkeepers, and aggregates actions by player and period. Only analytical summaries are published; raw event files are not redistributed.

What I did

I prepared player-and-period summaries in Python, designed the comparison controls and visualisations, added source-to-summary checks, and documented the limits of the comparison. The event collection and supplied xG model are StatsBomb’s work.

Metric definitions

  • Minutes and per 90: the union of lineup position intervals, measured against each period’s recorded event clock, including stoppage time. Lineup times have second-level resolution; these are not official nominal 90/120-minute totals. Temporary injury absences within lineup intervals are not deducted. Actions / minutes × 90; rates are hidden below 30 selected-period minutes. Pass completion remains completed / attempted.
  • Completed passes: pass events without a recorded outcome. Completion = completed / attempted passes. No attempts gives “—”.
  • Shots and goals: shot events and those with outcome Goal. The penalty checkbox changes shots, goals, xG and shot zones only.
  • xG: sum of StatsBomb’s supplied shot probabilities. It describes recorded chance quality. I did not train this model.
  • Successful dribbles: Dribble events with outcome Complete. Pressures: recorded Pressure events, not necessarily possession won.
  • Shot zones: source x = 80–100 or 100–120, each split into three equal y bands. The display rotates the attacking third so the goal is at the top. These are aggregate zones, not exact locations. Shots before x = 80 are counted separately beneath the pitch and in the table.

Checks and limits

Each match’s goals reconcile to its scoreline; the final reconciles to 30 shots and six goals (3–3), excluding shootouts. Period counts add back to match totals. Values are rounded only for display. Three selected knockout matches are an unrepresentative sample; this is descriptive analysis, not a prediction, player ranking or recruitment recommendation.
