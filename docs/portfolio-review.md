# Portfolio feedback checklist

Share https://tola-adelase.github.io/ with one recruiter, one data scientist and someone outside data. Ask each person to browse on their phone before explaining the site to them. Record their answers rather than adding testimonials without permission.

## Everyone: the first minute

1. After five seconds, what do you think I do and what kind of role am I moving towards?
2. Which project would you open first, and why?
3. Can you find my LinkedIn, GitHub and public CV?
4. Where did you hesitate, get lost or struggle to read something on your phone?

## Recruiter

- Is my current Data and Reporting Analyst experience clear?
- Does the evidence support a move towards Data Science? What would you still need to see?
- Which sentence sounds vague or overstated?

## Data scientist

- Can you distinguish my contribution from the supplied data, course brief and existing models?
- Are the methods, uncertainty and limitations understandable and defensible?
- Can you find the source code and see what was rerun versus reviewed from saved outputs?
- In Match Lab, is the minutes denominator clear, and do totals and per-90 views tell a consistent story?

## Someone outside data

- Can you explain one project back to me in your own words?
- Which chart or phrase needs a clearer explanation?
- What would make you want to keep exploring?

## Record and act

For each person, capture: date, perspective, device, confusing point, suggested change and priority. Fix repeated confusion first. Keep personal feedback private unless the reviewer agrees to publication.
