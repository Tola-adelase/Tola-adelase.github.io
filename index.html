03 / The person behind the data

Analytical by trade.
Curious by nature.

I like the part where a messy question starts to make sense.

I’m a Data and Reporting Analyst in Commercial and Sales Operations at Sage, Manchester/Newcastle. I build and maintain Power BI reporting, use SQL and DAX to prepare and model data, and help people understand commercial performance. The work starts well before a visual: agreeing what a measure means, checking the numbers and making the answer useful.

REPORTING IN PRACTICE / SAGE

Professional work. Private by design.

At Sage, I build reporting that brings together reliable data, consistent measures and a clear view of commercial performance.

These reports are confidential. This portfolio showcases my personal and academic projects.

I hold an MSc in Data Science and Computational Intelligence from Coventry University. My dissertation explored football player valuation using machine learning—a subject that connects my technical interests with my love of football.

I’m building on that foundation through Multiverse’s Data Fellowship Level 4, alongside my commercial analytics work. This portfolio brings together my personal and academic projects, with a focus on the questions, methods and judgement behind the work.

Away from the spreadsheets: Liverpool, films, music, anime, art and good food. Sometimes I write about the patterns that don’t fit neatly into a dataset.
