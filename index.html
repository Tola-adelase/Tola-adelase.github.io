03 / The person behind the data

Analytical by trade.
Curious by nature.

I enjoy turning a complicated question into something people can understand and act on.

I work as a Data Analyst in Commercial and Sales Operations at Sage. That experience gives me a practical perspective on data: start with the question, understand the context, and make the answer useful.
